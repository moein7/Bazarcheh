1. Review and approve essential ADRs.

3. Keep the four-hour time budget visible.
4. Require tests and completion reports.
5. Preserve all challenge contracts.
6. Integrate and independently verify.

Do not fabricate test results, model metrics, data labels, or AI logs. If a requirement cannot be safely resolved, explain the blocker and ask the user.
